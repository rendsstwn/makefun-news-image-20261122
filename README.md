Sum up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026 in one image. The entry program writes a 1024x1024 PNG digest of 30 stories from that day: COP31 collapses in Antalya, Iran strikes in Bahrain and tankers near the Strait of Hormuz, Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen, 186 drones over Ukraine, a missile hits Kryvyi Rih, the Sparta II ablaze in the Gulf of Finland, markets in correction, a cartel boss captured in Mexico, Verstappen wins in Las Vegas, and 21 more stories from around the world. Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, Al Jazeera, Helsinki Times, 22 Nov 2026.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, about 134 KB). The program copies `entry.png` from this repository, with no network, no randomness and no timestamps, so two runs write the same bytes.
